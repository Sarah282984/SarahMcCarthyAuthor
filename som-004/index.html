Her brother left her there with a bag of food and a promise. The food is gone. Something took it in the night, and would have taken Kel too. So she does the only thing she can. She asks the walnut trees to grow, and they grow. She asks the vines to climb, and they climb.

By the time Finn comes back, his six-year-old sister is living inside a green fortress of her own making, eating berries that have no business fruiting this early, talking to a ferret who talks back.

Finn is sixteen, which is the age magic comes. His came without warning and burned their village to the ground with their parents inside it.

A hundred years ago, magic broke this kingdom. What is left of it brands mages with a crescent moon and hangs them from trees along the road. In the city of Westwend, a man in black and silver has grown rich hunting them, and for years he has been opening mages up on a table, one after another, looking for the thing that makes them different from him.

He has never heard of a mage under sixteen.

Now he has.

Shadows of Magic is book one of a complete coming-of-age epic fantasy trilogy about the price of power, the people who pay it, and a brother and sister who only have each other.
